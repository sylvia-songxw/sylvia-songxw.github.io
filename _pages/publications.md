## Conference Proceedings

### [Stable Marriage on Networks](https://doi.org/10.65109/pkvj4128)

*Miao Li, Xinwei Song, Dengji Zhao* · AAMAS 2026 · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.65109/pkvj4128)

We study stable two-sided matching on social networks, where agents can invite others to join the game. We propose a mechanism in which participants are truthful about their preferences, and both sides have incentives to invite new participants.

An earlier version appeared at DAI 2025 as *Stable Marriages via Social Relationship* · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772429.3772443).

### [Designing Incentives for Networked Multi-agent Systems](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i48.42167)

*Xinwei Song* · AAAI 2026 Doctoral Consortium · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i48.42167) · [PDF](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/42167/46128)

A research agenda for achieving globally desirable outcomes in networked multi-agent systems, along two complementary paths: top-down mechanism design that guides strategic agents towards optimal equilibria, and bottom-up behavior emergence, where farsighted policies such as cooperation arise from multi-agent reinforcement learning.

### [Housing Market on Networks](https://doi.org/10.65109/dvvr5292)

*Xinwei Song, Tianyi Yang, Dengji Zhao* · AAMAS 2025 · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.65109/dvvr5292) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.09759)

We redefine stability and optimality for one-sided matching on networks and prove the best notions achievable together with incentive compatibility. We then propose Connected Trading Cycles, the first mechanism to attain them, alongside Swap With Neighbors and Leave and Share.

### [Truthful and Stable One-sided Matching on Networks](https://doi.org/10.65109/pekx6502)

*Tianyi Yang, Yuxiang Zhai, Dengji Zhao, Xinwei Song, Miao Li* · AAMAS 2024 (extended abstract), pp. 2564–2566 · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.65109/pekx6502) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.05787)

We propose Swap With Neighbors, a mechanism that works on any network structure and satisfies incentive compatibility together with the tightest notion of stability defined in this setting, and its natural improvement, Leave and Share.

## Preprints and Manuscripts Under Review

### Learning to Adapt through Reviews in Online Markets

*Xinwei Song et al.* · WWW 2027, under review

We model online markets with reviews, in which buyers learn both how to purchase and how to review, while sellers learn how to disclose information. When the reviews are visible to both sides, a forward-looking algorithm prevents market collapse and raises the average trade rate from 2% to 29.7%.

### [Learning to Cooperate with Emergent Reputation via Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04359)

*Xinwei Song, Yizhe Huang, Dengji Zhao, Xue Feng* · AAAI 2027, under review · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04359)

COOPER is a distributed MARL method that jointly learns reputation assessment rules, gossip over the social network, and policies entirely from environment rewards. A lookahead at optimization time shortens credit assignment, so cooperation and reputation norms co-emerge in self-play and adapt to different co-players.

### [NormAct: Benchmarking Embodied Agents' Proactive Compliance with Unspoken Social Norms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27826)

*Shiyun Zhao, Xinwei Song, Tianyu Guo, Xiaomeng Gao, Mingyuan Liu, Xu Han, Yuanyuan Zhang, Zhenliang Zhang, Xue Feng, Bo Dai* · ARR October 2026, under review · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27826)

A benchmark of 550 TongSim scenarios in which the same goal admits norm-compliant or norm-violating action sequences, with the applicable rule omitted from the instruction. Across MLLM planners, goal achievement (67.4%) far exceeds norm compliance (24.7%) without guidance, and a norm hinter trained on generated norm-understanding data lifts compliance substantially (e.g. Gemini 3 Pro from 19.5% to 46.0%).
